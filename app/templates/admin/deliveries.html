How it works

  • Waiting: paid, and booked automatically at the time shown.
  • Booked: Uber accepted it; the courier collects from the kitchen and delivers inside the slot.
  • Retrying: Uber refused or was unreachable; the bot tries again every 5 minutes until the slot ends.
  • Missed: the slot ended without a courier - arrange this delivery by hand.
  • Send to Uber now books straight away (use it to test, or if the automatic booking is stuck).
