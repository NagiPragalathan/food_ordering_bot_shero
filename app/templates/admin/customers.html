How the Zoho column and the buttons work

The Zoho column is checked against Zoho on every load: a record deleted there shows as Deleted in Zoho and its link is dropped, so the next push recreates it.

Push to Zoho sends the customer's details, address and Bot Stage to their Lead (their Contact once they have paid), converts a paid customer still held as a Lead, and files any paid order Zoho does not have yet.

Delete removes the customer here only - use it to clear test data; their next WhatsApp message starts from the beginning.
