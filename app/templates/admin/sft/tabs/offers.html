  1. Code: the lead sends the code on WhatsApp, alone or with SFT ("SFT20" or "SFT SFT20"). The bot confirms the new fee and carries on where they were. A code starts the journey even on Shero US's shared number.
  2. Automatic: no code; it covers everyone (or the sources ticked) between its dates. Good for a launch price.

  4. The follow-up templates are approved by WhatsApp with fixed words, so they keep saying the full fee; the lead sees their price when they tap Enroll.
