What you will work toward

  • Understand the preparation and operating topics taught in the course.
  • Follow the assigned standardized recipe, including gram and spoon measures where provided.
  • Prepare the required base items (such as OTM base, Toor Dal base or HDM-2 base masala, as assigned) and submit them through the app for approval before cooking finished products.
  • Cook the products assigned for your selected cuisine and upload product photos through the app for review.
